About

A video streaming experience for public-domain movies. Public-domain cinema is a strange, sprawling library: forgotten classics, weird experiments, and films whose copyright lapsed by accident or design. Every film here is free for anyone to watch and share. You can also drag and drop your own local video and subtitle files to use this as a private, offline media player.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The built-in catalogue is sourced from archive.org and publicdomainmovie.net.

Everything runs directly in your browser: the catalogue, the search index, and the resume-watching memory. There is no backend, no server-side processing, and absolutely no tracking.

Wikipedia in the subtitles

Have you ever paused a movie to look up a historical figure, an obscure event, or a complex concept mentioned in the dialogue? To save you the trouble, every film in this catalogue has been enriched with hidden Wikipedia annotations to provide instant context.

The Q and W shortcuts

Press Q at any point during playback to reveal markers on the progress bar for every annotated subject. Press W to instantly pause the film and open the Wikipedia article closest to your current timestamp.

Drop movies to play

Drag and drop one or more video files anywhere on this window — they'll be added to a playlist. Works best with .mp4 and .webm. .mkv may work depending on the codec.

Space play/pause   ←/→ seek 10s   ⌘+←/⌘+→ prev / next track   ↑/↓ volume   M mute   F fullscreen   C captions   Q Wikipedia   ,/. speed   0–9 jump to %  

Subtitles: drop .srt or .vtt files alongside videos — paired by filename. Wikipedia: any URL inside an SRT cue is captured as an annotation. Press Q anywhere to pause and open the closest one. ⌘+Shift+R (or Ctrl+Shift+R) wipes saved state.
